# Lean proofs

The statements of the study that Lean 4 and Mathlib can check today: the identities of the Mukai lattice and of central charges, the integer arithmetic of walls with two spherical simples, and the point-mass corollary

  lim m_σ(Φ^{±n} 𝒪_x)^{1/n} = exp h_cat(Φ) = exp h_cat(Φ⁻¹),

derived from a stated list of geometric inputs. Lean 4.36.0, Mathlib `v4.36.0`, no `sorry`, no axiom beyond Lean's three standard ones.

The statements follow the research draft *Harder–Narasimhan towers of spherical twist words on a Picard-rank-one K3 surface*; references to it are by name, because its numbering is still changing.

## Build

Needs [elan](https://github.com/leanprover/elan); it installs the pinned toolchain on first use.

```bash
make lean-deps   # once: Mathlib's build cache, several GB
```

```bash
make lean        # build (any warning fails it), audit the axioms, check the model
```

## What is proved from stated inputs

`PointMassInputs` lists what the corollary uses about `D^b(X)`. Mathlib does not yet have derived categories of coherent sheaves on a K3 surface, Bridgeland stability or categorical entropy, so these are hypotheses (fields), each with its source in its docstring:

- Serre duality `h(U, V) = h(V, U)`, invariance of total Hom under autoequivalences, additivity on `G = S₀ ⊕ S₁ ⊕ S₂`;
- the diagonal estimate `h(U, V) ≤ K h(U, G) h(G, V)` (Orlov, arXiv:0804.1163, Theorem 4, with regularity for `K`);
- the point test `h(E, 𝒪_x) = R(E)` and `ν R(E) ≤ h(E, G)` for spherical `E`, and `m_τ ≍ h(G, ·)` (the article's point-measurement lemma);
- Lemma A.1 (for spherical `E`) and Lemma A.2 of the article;
- standard words: their powers are standard, they move points to shifted points, their `H_n` grows quadratically;
- Bridgeland's metric bound on masses (arXiv:math/0212237, Proposition 8.1), the action on stability conditions, positive masses;
- the entropy formula for smooth proper categories (Dimitrov–Haiden–Katzarkov–Kontsevich, arXiv:1307.8418, Theorem 2.6).

From these, Lean proves every assertion of the corollary:

| Assertion | Lean |
|---|---|
| `c e^{-d(σ,τ)} p(W) ≤ m_σ(W𝒪_x) ≤ C e^{d(σ,τ)} p(W)` for every word | `PointMassInputs.uniform_estimate` |
| Arbitrary prefixes: point mass and `p(W_n)` have the same rates when `d(σ_n, τ) = o(n)` | `PointMassInputs.tendsto_log_mass_div_sub` |
| Comparison for a fixed period: `H(W) ≤ J p(W)` for every `W` commuting with a nonstandard `Φ` | `PointMassInputs.exists_H_le_mul_p` |
| `H(Φ^{m+n}) ≤ K H(Φ^m) H(Φ^n)`, `H(W⁻¹) = H(W)`, `h_cat(Φ⁻¹) = h_cat(Φ)` | `PointMassInputs.H_pow_add_le`, `H_inv`, `hcat_inv` |
| Certified upper bounds `(K H(Φ^k))^{1/k}` converging to `exp h_cat(Φ)` | `PointMassInputs.rate_certified` |
| The limit, uniformly in `x` and in `σ` with `d(σ, τ) ≤ D₀`; for `Φ⁻ⁿ`; along `σ_n` with `d(σ_n, τ) = o(n)` and any `x_n` | `PointMassInputs.tendstoUniformly_pointMass`, `tendsto_pointMass`, `tendsto_pointMass_inv`, `tendsto_log_pointMass_of_sublinear` |
| The rate per letter `exp(h_cat(Φ)/ℓ)` along the prefixes of a periodic word | `PointMassInputs.tendsto_log_pointMass_prefix` |

A theorem proved from hypotheses that cannot hold together says nothing. [tests/lean/Consistency.lean](../../tests/lean/Consistency.lean) builds a five-object model in which every field holds. [tests/lean/AxiomAudit.lean](../../tests/lean/AxiomAudit.lean) checks every declaration in the `K3Twists` namespace for `sorry` and for axioms other than `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`.

## What this does not cover

The Harder–Narasimhan tower algorithm, visibility on the cover, the wall lemmas beyond their integer arithmetic, and the evaluation lemma are not formalized; neither is the higher-Picard-rank work. They need derived categories of K3 surfaces and stability conditions in Mathlib first.
